About Shippsy

Shippsy is a leading manufacturer of high-quality sports apparel, dedicated to innovation, sustainability, and ethical practices. With a rich history spanning over two decades, we've consistently delivered exceptional products that meet the rigorous demands of athletes and sports enthusiasts worldwide.

Our History

2002: Founded with a Vision

Shippsy was established with a vision to revolutionize sports apparel manufacturing.

Expanded Global Reach

Expanded operations to serve a global clientele, establishing key partnerships.

Introduced Sustainable Materials

Pioneered the use of eco-friendly materials, setting new industry standards.

Launched Innovation Hub

Launched a dedicated innovation hub to drive cutting-edge product development.

Our Mission

To empower athletes and sports enthusiasts with innovative, high-performance apparel that enhances their experience and supports their goals, while upholding the highest standards of sustainability and ethical responsibility.
